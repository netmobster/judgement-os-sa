The ledger row (figure · claim · tag) — the unit of proof. Stack them wherever numbers appear; adjacent rows space themselves.

```jsx
<EvidenceRow figure="7 of 7" claim="Heartbeat end to end on staging" state="measured" tagLabel="measured · staging" />
<EvidenceRow figure="Local" claim="Nobody has done it yet" state="hypothesis" />
```

Real rows sit on white with a shadow; unproven rows (`open`, automatic for hypothesis) sit flat on surface. Certainty is in the ground, not the tag hue.
